About SafeGuard Network

We are a global organization dedicated to protecting women and children from violence, exploitation, and abuse.

Our Mission

SafeGuard Network works tirelessly to create a world where women and children can live free from fear, violence, and exploitation. We provide emergency support, legal assistance, and long-term rehabilitation services.

Founded in 2008, we've expanded our reach to serve vulnerable populations in over 80 countries, working with local communities, governments, and international organizations.

Our comprehensive approach includes prevention programs, crisis intervention, survivor support, and advocacy for policy changes that protect women and children's rights.
